I'm a master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University. My research focuses on tactile sensing and multimodal robot learning, from building sensors and data pipelines to training manipulation policies.

At Johns Hopkins, I work on shear-force sensing and event-based visuo-tactile imitation learning. During my 2026 internship at Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence, I helped develop HACo, a haptic approach to force-aware dexterous manipulation.

My earlier work spans neural decoding, biosignal analysis, FPGA systems, and language-model fine-tuning. I'm preparing to apply for Fall 2027 PhD programs.
